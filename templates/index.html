How the Two-Stage Re-Ranking & Generation Pipeline Works

Standard RAG systems pollute context windows by dumping hundreds of noisy chunks into LLMs. Our pipeline applies dense vector retrieval, cross-attention re-ranking, and mathematical verification:

1. Qdrant Vector Search

Extracts PDFs with pypdf, chunks text with overlapping windows, and embeds using Google's gemini-embedding-001 into isolated Qdrant vector collections.

2. Cross-Encoder Re-Ranking

Stage 1 pulls 20 broad candidate chunks. Stage 2 evaluates full token-level cross-attention via ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2 to compress down to the 5 highest-signal chunks.

3. Structured Pydantic Output

Gemini is bound to strict Pydantic schemas (MockTestPaper) with Few-Shot prompts to guarantee zero structural hallucinations and structured question outputs.

4. Critic / Verifier Audit

An automated audit loop verifies that question marks mathematically sum to the requested total, validates syllabus boundaries, and confirms partial-credit solution rubrics for every question.
